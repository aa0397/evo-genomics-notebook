1. For the four floral traits (ltype, lobe, callus, mentum), does ER or ARD receive higher AIC weight? What does that tell you about whether floral structures are gained and lost at similar rates?
  
  For labellum type, labellum lobes, and callus, equal rates had the highest AIC weight.
  For mentum, all rates different had the highest AIC weight. 
  This tells us than generall these floral structure are gained at lost at similar rates. Mentum may be under some sort of pollinator or sexual selection or selection from heat or humidity or something dependent on orchid habitat 
  
2. Looking at the stochastic character map for troph, how many independent times did full mycoheterotrophy (HM, state 2) evolve from partial mycoheterotrophy (PM, state 1) in Corallorhiza?

  Mycoheterotrophy evolved only twice in Corallorhiza from the partial state. There is a small chance it evolved directly from the non-mycoheterotroph state.
  
3. What was the most likely trophic mode for the ancestor or Corallorhiza? What about the Corallorhiza striata and C. maculata species complexes?

  The most likely trophic mode for the ancestor of Corallorhiza was non-mycoheterotrophic. It would have gotten all of its nutrients from photosynthesis and the soil.
